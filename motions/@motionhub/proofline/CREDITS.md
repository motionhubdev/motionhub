# Credits

## Original work

Proofline is an original motion. Its visual system, semantic connectors, mathematical treatments, documentation, and component designs are created for this project. It is not based on another Motion Hub motion and currently declares no `basedOn` relationship.

## Fonts

The motion requires the following open-licence font families. The font stacks in `tokens.json` also name local system fallbacks; those fallback fonts are not bundled or redistributed with the motion.

- **Manrope** — headings, body copy, captions, and short explanations.
  - Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
  - Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  - Licence text: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt
- **STIX Two Math** — mathematical notation, variables, expressions, and coordinates.
  - Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/stixtwomath
  - Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  - Licence text: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/stixtwomath/OFL.txt
- **IBM Plex Mono** — metadata, view names, and short annotations.
  - Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono
  - Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  - Licence text: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ibmplexmono/OFL.txt

## Images, sound, and other assets

No external image, video, sound, model, or other asset is required by the words and tokens alone. `poster.jpg` and the five images in `react/previews/` are original frames or crops from this project's Remotion compositions. No stock imagery is used. `react/sound.json` and the reel's `cues` define original programmatic sound cues synthesised by `@motionhub/runtime/sound`; no recorded music, stock audio, or external sound assets are included.
