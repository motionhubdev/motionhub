None. The paper's tooth, the wood's grain and the uneven ink are generated noise drawn by the components.

## Words in the reel

The quotation and the figures of the house are from Henry David Thoreau, *Walden* (1854), in the public domain: the chapters "Where I Lived, and What I Lived For" and "Economy". The checklist is written for this motion.
