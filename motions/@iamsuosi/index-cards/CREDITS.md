# Credits

Index Cards is an original motion by SuoSi. The cards, the desk, the stamp, the components and the drawings in its reel were made for it.

## Fonts

- Zilla Slab, by Typotheque for Mozilla: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Zilla+Slab
- Karla, by Jonny Pinhorn: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Karla
- Noto Serif SC and Noto Sans SC, by Google: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/noto

The faces are not shipped in the motion. Players take the Latin files from Google Fonts; the Noto faces are named for Chinese and are not bundled.

## Assets

None. The linen weave is drawn with gradients, the stamp's ink with generated noise, and the plant, pots and watering can in the reel are lines drawn in its code.

## Facts in the reel

The repotting steps and the advice to choose a pot about 2 to 5 cm wider are common gardening guidance.
