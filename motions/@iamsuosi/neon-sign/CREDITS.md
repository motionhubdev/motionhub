None. The bricks, the grit and the glow are drawn by the components.

## Words in the reel

The reel's mark and its words "after hours" were made for this motion and name nothing. Its facts are general history: neon was found in London in 1898, by William Ramsay and Morris Travers; a lamp of it was first shown in Paris in 1910, by Georges Claude; pure neon glows red-orange.
