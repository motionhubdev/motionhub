# Credits

Receipt Roll is an original motion by SuoSi. The paper, the printer's mouth, the bar pattern, the ballpoint strokes and the code were drawn for this motion.

## Fonts

- Courier Prime, by Alan Dague-Greene: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Courier+Prime
- Work Sans, by Wei Huang: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Work+Sans
- Noto Sans SC, by Google: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+SC

The faces are not shipped in the motion. Players take the Latin files from Google Fonts; Noto Sans SC is named for Chinese and is not bundled.

## Assets

None. The counter's grain is generated noise, and the bars under a total are a pattern of the motion's own: they encode nothing.

## Reel

The reel's figures are standard reference values: the composition of dry air by volume, and the freezing point, average body temperature and boiling point of water in Celsius and Fahrenheit.
